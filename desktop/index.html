Does the ladder hold up out of sample?

Protocol (to avoid overfitting): the rules are frozen at your current Backtest settings before anything runs. They're tested on two survivorship-free coin lists — the top 30 by market cap (no stablecoins/wrapped tokens) at each cycle's peak, taken from CoinMarketCap's snapshots of 7 Jan 2018 and 7 Nov 2021. That includes coins that later collapsed (LUNA, FTT, BitConnect…), so the result can't be flattered by only testing today's winners. Each list runs from just after its peak: Feb 2018 → Dec 2021 and Nov 2021 → today. Benchmarks: buy & hold from the window start, and DCA ($1,000 split over 24 monthly buys). $1,000 per coin, 0.1% fee, daily closes.
Data: DefiLlama daily prices (covers dead coins) + alternative.me Fear & Greed, pulled into memory when you press Run and dropped when you close the page. Nothing is written to disk.
≈ 1–2 minutes · ~60 coin histories

Backtest the fear strategy

Daily OHLC from Bybit (USDT; Kraken fallback, ZEC from Kraken ≈2y), XMR history from KuCoin embedded in xmr-history.js (starts 2021-03), Fear & Greed from alternative.me. Signals are read at the daily close and filled at the next open. Stops and take-profits fill intrabar. Fees are applied per side. Historical simulation, not a forecast. Each sweep changes one setting at a time and holds the rest fixed; the best values are not a joint optimum.

Per-asset triggers
Buy: enter when price ≤ level or F&G ≤ global buy. Add: second tranche when price ≤ level (only if below entry). Bail: thesis exit when price ≤ level (only if below entry). Target: full exit at gain %. Blank = not used. ZEC and HYPE are watch-only; levels are illustrative. Fixed price levels are current-cycle levels; applying them to older history is illustrative, not a historical signal. Trigger edits and toggles apply on the next Run.
